The Concierge

You have secured your stay, now build your itinerary.

Westfield now builds the days around the stay, not just the stay itself — walking, mountain biking, sailing, water skiing, climbing and everything else the Lake District does well, arranged for our own guests and for anyone else who'd like Ullswater organised properly.

The other half of the stay

Westfield, The Experience.

Stay Westfield is still where this begins — comfortable beds, a hot tub, a terrace over Ullswater worth getting up for. If you haven't booked yet, that's the place to start.

The Concierge picks up from there. Once your dates are set — with us, or anywhere else on the lake — we build the days around them: walking, biking, sailing, climbing and everything else the Lake District does well, matched to your ability, your appetite for risk, and how many mornings you actually want to be up before eight.

Have a look through the modules below, see what a few weeks look like already put together, then tell us what you're after. Every activity is run by the people who do it professionally, day in, day out — our part is knowing who to call, in what order, and around what weather.

The route we take.

What's already on our doorstep.

A working network of outdoor specialists — some on the lakeshore, others a short drive through Keswick or up towards Blencathra.

  • Glenridding Sailing CentreSailing · kayaking

    RYA tuition and boat hire from a lakeshore base at the southern end of Ullswater.

  • Whinlatter Forest ParkMountain biking

    England's only true mountain forest, with waymarked red and black trails and bike hire on site.

  • Honister Slate MineVia ferrata

    Cabled via ferrata routes across an old slate quarry face at the top of Honister Pass.

  • Rookin House Activity CentreLand & speed

    Quad biking, off-roading, horse riding, archery and clay pigeon shooting, a few miles from Ullswater.

Plus the wake and ski operators at Pooley Bridge, the heritage steamers, Keswick's climbing and ghyll specialists, and a handful of more unusual finds — falconry, alpaca trekking, dark-sky nights and local tastings among them.

A few questions

Before you enquire.

Do we have to stay at Westfield to use the concierge?

No. The concierge service is open to anyone staying anywhere on Ullswater — our own guests just get it as standard.

How far in advance should we book activities?

As soon as your dates are fixed, ideally. Popular sessions — via ferrata, sailing tuition, ghyll scrambling — get booked up in peak season, so the earlier we know your plans the more choice we have.

What happens if the weather turns?

We build a wet-weather alternative into every itinerary from the start, and stay in touch with each operator on the day. Nobody's left standing in a field wondering what's happening.

Can you organise activities for large or mixed-ability groups?

Yes — it's one of the things we spend the most time on. Most of the Lake District's operators are used to running sessions for a wide age and ability range within a single booking.

Is there an extra cost for the concierge service itself?

Every itinerary is tailored specifically to your trip, so scope and cost vary accordingly. Tell us what you're after using the enquiry form below and we'll confirm exactly what's involved before anything's booked.
